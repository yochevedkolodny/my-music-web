Help & Feedback

What can we help you with?

Common questions

How do I import songs?

Tap the + icon on Home or Library and select audio files from your iPhone.

How do I add songs to a playlist?

Open a personal playlist, then choose Add Songs. Built-in smart playlists update automatically.

Where are my songs stored?

The current web app stores imported audio locally in your browser on this device. Keep your original files and a backup.

How do I back up my library?

Open Profile → Library backup, then save the generated ZIP to Files. Keep the ZIP somewhere safe.

Why does music sometimes stop in the background?

iOS may restrict background playback in web apps. The native beta is being developed to improve this.

Send Feedback

Your music files and listening history are never attached automatically. Feedback is addressed to yochevedlatest@gmail.com and opens your email app. You must press Send in your email app; this is not an automatic submission. Screenshots cannot be attached automatically to the email.
